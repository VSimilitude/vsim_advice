Put most of your early resources into Squad 1. Spreading shards, EXP, medals, and gear across three squads usually leaves you with three mediocre teams instead of one useful one.

Don’t automatically collect completed radar tasks. Stack them so you can cash them in on the VS days that award radar points.

Join the strongest active alliance that will take you. The alliance gifts, rallies, event rewards, buffs, and experienced players can accelerate your account dramatically.

In Desert Storm, troop survival matters more than the scoreboard after five minutes. Burning through your army early can make you irrelevant when the valuable middle objectives open.

For low spenders, tanks are usually the easiest first squad to develop. The game gives you a relatively accessible path to a coherent tank lineup early on.

Gold becomes enormously important for T10 research. Don’t reach HQ30 assuming your gold problems are over—they may just be beginning.

Keep unopened resource chests in your inventory until you need them. Inventory resources can’t be plundered the same way exposed base resources can.

If you have two Tech Centers, keep both running. A common approach is one long research and one shorter research that can be timed around events.

Don’t chase first place in every Arms Race. Getting the useful milestone chests consistently is often a much better resource trade than trying to win every bracket.

Don’t neglect radar gear just because it doesn’t increase the flashy attack number. Later-game players frequently value damage reduction and survivability extremely highly.

On VS building day, finish buildings rather than merely starting them. Pre-build upgrades beforehand and leave the “gift box” unopened until the scoring day when possible.

Rush HQ selectively rather than leveling every building evenly. Many players upgrade primarily the prerequisites needed for the next HQ level.

Save universal legendary shards for heroes that matter to your main squad. Early accounts rarely have enough shards to star every UR hero simultaneously.

Use Arms Race as a source of skill medals, not just leaderboard glory. Veteran players continue farming the milestone chests because skill medals remain scarce far into the game.

Don’t let completed radar storage hit its cap before the next refresh. If your queue is full, you can lose the benefit of additional refresh cycles.

For traditional formations, put durable defenders in front and damage dealers/support behind. A common early template is two defensive heroes front, three offensive/support heroes rear.

Experienced players often favor the 10,000-diamond 30-day VIP activation. Community reports repeatedly describe it as one of the better uses of freely earned diamonds.

Desert Storm hospitals matter because armies eventually run out. An alliance that can replenish troops while the opponent cannot gains a major endurance advantage.

Don’t indiscriminately upgrade green gear. Community guides recommend saving meaningful gear investment for higher rarities.

Time long research so it completes on VS research day. You get the research you wanted anyway, plus event credit for the completed tech power.

Kimberly is commonly treated as an early tank priority. Several beginner guides recommend pushing her star level before spreading legendary shards broadly.

You do not need to complete every new Honorable Campaign stage immediately. If you’re likely to fail the next levels, farming an older stage can produce more reliable rewards.

In Desert Storm, organize people into actual teams. Five independent strong players wandering around are often less useful than coordinated groups assigned to objectives.

Alliance Duel tech has unusually high strategic value. Unlocking additional VS reward chests increases the payoff from activities you were already going to perform.

Try to combine VS tasks with the matching Arms Race phase. One speedup or resource expenditure can then advance two reward systems simultaneously.

Save survivor recruitment tickets for the VS day that scores survivor recruitment. Randomly using them during the week sacrifices essentially free event points.

Don’t confuse Drone Data Chip chests with Drone Component chests. They score on different VS days once the relevant drone systems unlock.

A high-power squad can still lose to a lower displayed-power squad with better tech and gear. Raw squad power is an imperfect measure of combat strength.

Don’t leave valuable resources sitting exposed just because you have them. Open resource boxes when the upgrade actually requires them.

For most non-whales entering Season 1, Engineer is a popular profession choice. Its research, construction, and economic bonuses improve long-term account development.

War Leader makes more sense when combat contribution is genuinely your job. The more powerful and battle-focused the account, the more valuable its military bonuses become.

Before VS Day 1, send your squads gathering Sunday night. Time their return for after reset so the gathered resources count toward the new day.

Don’t spend legendary blueprints casually. Gear breakpoints at two and four stars often matter enough that random one-star upgrades can delay stronger bonuses.

The current late-game community meta heavily favors defensive mythic gear. Radars and then armor are frequently recommended before mythic chips and many mythic guns.

Be careful with mythic chip upgrades. Several experienced players consider the chip’s mythic benefit relatively weak compared with radar or armor.

Legendary gear should generally be crafted rather than lower-tier gear. Lower rarities arrive naturally from gameplay, so crafting materials are better saved for gold gear.

Use your strongest Tech Center survivors when you start research. Community testing says their bonuses only need to be active when the research begins, allowing advanced players to move them between centers.

Secretary of Science is worth planning around for very long research. Starting major research while holding the title can save substantial time.

Don’t simply attack whoever is nearest in Desert Storm. Killing troops is useful when it helps your team control objectives; farming kills while ignoring buildings can still lose the match.

Lower-power Desert Storm players can still be extremely useful. Capture undefended objectives, reinforce buildings, and force stronger enemies to waste movement chasing you.

Save hero recruitment tickets for hero-scoring days. Recruitment is much more valuable when the same ticket also earns VS or Arms Race rewards.

Don’t dump massive amounts of Hero EXP just to finish an Arms Race phase. Players often use recruitment tickets or cheap upgrades on low-level heroes to reach the milestones more efficiently.

Plan your account months ahead, especially as F2P. A resource mistake that barely matters to a whale can cost a free player weeks.

For F2P progression, a widely suggested long-term route is HQ30 → T10 → Age of Oil → HQ35. The exact timing varies, but the principle is to chase meaningful unlocks rather than cosmetic power.

Upgrade Squad 1 tech instead of spreading combat research indiscriminately. Focused research complements the same concentrated-resource strategy used for heroes and gear.

Special Forces research eventually becomes one of the major account bottlenecks. Start respecting its gold and Valor Badge requirements well before you reach T10.

Use Valor Badges primarily where they advance important tech and score VS points. Alliance Duel and Special Forces are common priorities, with Intercity Truck sometimes used as a cheaper filler.

Don’t refresh every radar task looking for perfection. The cumulative value of consistently completing them is usually more important than optimizing each individual reward.

Radar tasks contribute more than personal loot. Some provide clue progress needed by the alliance for activities such as Zombie Siege.

Save drone parts earlier than you think you need to. Players commonly regret arriving at later drone levels without having accumulated enough.

Don’t judge drone investment only by the drone’s displayed level. Components, chips, data, and other drone systems contribute significantly to actual combat performance.

In Desert Storm, secure mobility early. The Science Center’s teleport cooldown benefit lets your alliance react to fights much faster.

When the central Desert Storm objectives open, priorities change. Arsenal, Mercenary Factory, and Nuclear Silo become much more consequential than quietly holding low-value territory.

Don’t keep smashing into a Desert Storm position you cannot take. Rotate, pressure another objective, and make the opponent choose where to respond.

Attendance may matter more than your elaborate Desert Storm plan. Twenty coordinated participants usually have an enormous advantage over a nominally stronger roster with no-shows.

Voice coordination is disproportionately powerful in Desert Storm. Real-time calls allow players to teleport, reinforce, retreat, or switch objectives faster than text chat.

Your strongest Desert Storm players don’t necessarily belong stacked together. Distributing strength among several teams can prevent the opponent from owning the rest of the map.

Don’t leave your wall defended automatically during every attack. If the opponent massively outclasses you, removing defenders can avoid unnecessary troop casualties.

Remember that burning bases lose durability over time. Letting the fire continue can eventually cause your base to relocate.

Higher troop tiers are a major real-power upgrade. Barracks progression and ultimately T10 matter more than many superficial sources of account power.

Consider delaying Operation Falcon Special Ops. The free troops awarded scale to your highest trainable troop tier, so doing them later can yield higher-level soldiers.

Restart a Falcon Special Op if too many troops die before completion. The surviving troop count influences how many free troops you receive.

Save Secret Orders when you don’t need them immediately. Legendary Secret Missions become especially useful when an event rewards dispatching them.

Likewise, save Trade Contracts for strategic rerolls. A legendary truck dispatched on the appropriate VS day is worth much more than a random low-rarity truck.

The best upgrade is often one you finish on the right day. Last War heavily rewards delaying collection or completion by a few hours to align with VS and Arms Race.

Don’t blindly level every resource building while rushing HQ. Their payback periods can be long, so weigh production gains against immediate progression requirements.

On the other hand, don’t permanently abandon resource production either. Late-game players point out that production multipliers, survivors, alliance bonuses, and Engineer skills can make upgraded mines worthwhile over time.

Gold mines deserve special consideration. Gold demand for research becomes severe enough that players who ignored production sometimes regret it near T10.

Your hero upgrade priority is more than hero level. Star rank, skills, exclusive weapons, gear, and team composition can matter more than simply pouring in EXP.

Community advice commonly prioritizes star upgrades before excessive hero leveling. A high-level low-star hero may contribute less than the displayed level suggests.

Use purple universal shards strategically too. Early tank players often invest them in heroes such as Mason, partly because of later seasonal progression opportunities.

Don’t assume the same gear path is optimal forever. Tank, air, and missile matchups—and the squads common on your server—can change whether armor or radar gives you more immediate value.

Read your combat reports. If your squad consistently dies to physical damage, for example, investing in the defensive stat that addresses that problem may beat following a generic spreadsheet.

Mythic gear effects matter, not merely the extra stats. This is one reason experienced players often rank mythic radar much higher than its raw power increase would imply.

Don’t turn every gun mythic just because your DPS hero is important. The mythic gun effect is matchup-dependent, while defensive gear helps across broader matchups.

Get useful gear to level 40 before creating a wildly uneven equipment set. Several community upgrade paths establish a solid legendary baseline before chasing expensive star/mythic progression.

When using regular blueprints, think in two-star and four-star milestones. Those breakpoints often provide more meaningful bonuses than stopping at odd star levels.

Don’t dismantle or restructure gear casually without checking what is refunded. Some resets return ore and blueprints but not every resource you invested.

Save Hero EXP for VS hero day when practical. The same EXP produces the same stronger hero, but now it also earns alliance points.

Save Skill Medals for VS hero day too. This is one of the easiest examples of turning normal account progression into additional event value.

Exclusive Weapon shards can also be timed around scoring events. Unless the power increase is urgently needed for a battle, patience can generate extra rewards.

On research day, use research speedups only after checking Arms Race timing. If Tech Research phase is coming soon, waiting can make those minutes score twice.

The same principle applies to construction speedups. City Building Arms Race plus VS construction day is much more efficient than random speedup dumping.

Don’t spend stamina without considering the calendar. Stamina can generate VS points during appropriate days while still producing loot from Doom Elites and rallies.

Some experienced low-spend players buy extra stamina with diamonds daily. The justification is the accumulated loot from Doom Elite farming, although this competes with other diamond priorities.

Winning an Arms Race bracket is optional; opening the milestone boxes is the reliable value. The system rewards consistent planning more than occasional all-in pushes.

Check the Arms Race calendar before consuming anything scarce. Knowing tomorrow’s phases lets you hold buildings, tech, troop upgrades, drone resources, and recruitment for a few hours instead of wasting overlap.

Promotion of lower-tier troops can be useful for troop-training events. You can time upgrades of existing soldiers rather than always training a completely fresh batch.

In Desert Storm, teleport away from a fight you cannot survive. Preserving thousands of troops can be more valuable than stubbornly defending one position.

Adjust who is defending your wall during Desert Storm. Strong versus weak opponents call for different defensive choices, and blindly leaving squads home can burn unnecessary troops.

Use substitutes in Desert Storm. Once a participant is essentially troopless, replacing them with a fresh rostered player gives your side usable combat strength again.

Small groups can snipe undefended Desert Storm buildings. Even if they cannot beat the enemy’s main force, they can force that force to move and surrender pressure elsewhere.

Don’t confuse personal Desert Storm MVP points with helping the alliance win. Hunting kills can score very well personally while contributing less than objective control.

Use raids on enemy bases strategically in Desert Storm. Depleting the troops of major enemy players is valuable because an army with no soldiers cannot contest objectives effectively.

Your alliance should decide beforehand who handles what in Desert Storm. Assigned hospitals, roaming attackers, support players, and objective teams reduce the chaos after the match begins.

Don’t overcommit to a single Desert Storm objective. Holding 51% of the valuable map consistently can be better than concentrating everyone in one glorious fight.

Progress toward T10 is partly a resource-management problem, not merely a timer problem. Special Forces research consumes so much gold that careless spending elsewhere can become the actual bottleneck.

The second Tech Center is frequently singled out as one of the best small purchases. Unlike many temporary bundles, it increases the amount of research work your account can perform continuously.

The second builder gets similar praise from low spenders. Permanent additional build capacity tends to age better than a one-time resource pack.

The meta-rule behind a lot of Last War strategy is simple: don’t consume something just because you can. Radar tasks, shards, speedups, recruitment tickets, buildings, research, stamina, drone materials, and gear upgrades become substantially more valuable when you use them at the right event window.
